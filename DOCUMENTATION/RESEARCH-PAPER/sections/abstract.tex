\begin{abstract}
The Automotive Diagnostic and Activity Monitoring (ADAM) system is a comprehensive platform engineered to deliver real-time diagnostics and behavioral insights for four-wheeled vehicles. Designed as both a dynamic monitoring tool and a forensic black box, ADAM integrates multiple sensing modules—including GPS, MPU6050 (gyroscope and accelerometer), and a vibration sensor (SW-420)—to capture vehicular dynamics with high fidelity. Central to the system is its interface with the vehicle’s Electronic Control Unit (ECU) via the On-Board Diagnostics (OBD-II) port, enabling access to critical engine parameters and diagnostic trouble codes. Sensor data is persistently recorded onto local storage (SD card) and synchronized with a PostgreSQL database for further processing.

Leveraging this multi-source dataset, ADAM employs a suite of machine learning models for four primary functions: (1) engine health assessment, (2) fuel consumption analysis, (3) driver behavior profiling, and (4) predictive maintenance. Results are rendered through a user-centric mobile application featuring dynamic graphs and charts, offering intuitive visualization of diagnostic metrics. Additionally, an integrated AI-powered chatbot enhances system interactivity by assisting users in interpreting results and suggesting corrective actions. The ADAM system exemplifies a scalable and intelligent automotive analytics solution, promoting vehicle safety, operational efficiency, and data-driven decision-making for both drivers and fleet operators.
\end{abstract}
